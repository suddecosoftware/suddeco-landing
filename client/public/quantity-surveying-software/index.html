In short

Suddeco is quantity surveying software for UK contractors and surveyors. It generates an automated bill of quantities and priced takeoff from a drawing — structured as areas, stages, tasks and materials — and keeps the surveyor in control of every rate, margin and revision. From £49/month.

What you get

Everything from the drawing to a client-ready, priced scope of works — in one connected workspace.

Automated bill of quantities

Suddeco builds a structured BoQ from the drawing — areas → stages → tasks → materials — so you start from a complete, priced document, not a blank sheet.

Priced takeoff, live rates

Quantities are priced against current UK labour and material costs, giving you a cost plan that reflects today's prices.

Your rates, your margins

Maintain a rate library, then override globally or per project, stage or line. The automation never overrides your professional judgement.

Defensible and structured

Every figure traces back to a measured quantity and a rate, so your BoQ stands up to scrutiny from clients and main contractors.

Signed client pack

Revisions and versions

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What does Suddeco do for quantity surveyors?

Suddeco automates the measurement and pricing behind a bill of quantities. It takes off the drawing, builds a structured BoQ, and prices it against live UK rates — so the surveyor spends time on judgement, checking and negotiation rather than manual measurement.

Is the bill of quantities structured?

Yes. Suddeco structures every job as areas, stages, tasks and materials, giving you a clear, defensible cost plan rather than a single lump-sum figure.

Can I use my own rates?

Yes. Keep a rate library and override pricing globally or per project, stage or line. You stay in full control of the numbers.

Does it handle revisions?

Yes. Suddeco tracks revisions and versions so you can see how the BoQ and cost plan changed between issues.

Can I produce a client-ready document?

Yes. Export a branded, signed scope of works and cost plan straight from the priced BoQ, ready for the client to approve.
